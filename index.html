01 / The platform

Connect the work.
Keep the child in view.

When advice, plans and reviews sit apart, the whole case is harder to see. Sensol connects the information and the work around it.

01 / DRAFT

Individual evidence.
Individualised plans.

Develop EHCP drafts from the child’s evidence, using SEND-specific language and source links for professionals to check, edit and approve.

  • Each sentence links to its report and page
  • Gaps in the evidence are flagged, not filled
02 / COORDINATE

Shared casework.
Connected expertise.

Bring professional contributions, advice requests and responsibilities together, with routine follow-ups carried through.

  • Every six-week advice period tracked
  • Unresolved requests escalated to your team
03 / TRACK

Provision connected
to progress.

Record support delivered and progress against outcomes, giving the next review a clearer evidence base.

  • Section F provision carried into the review
  • Progress recorded against Section E outcomes

Sensol prepares advice requests from approved templates, monitors due dates and follows up on your agreed schedule. When advice arrives, it attaches the response and updates the case. Requests that reach agreed limits are referred to your team with their history.

Your team retains professional judgement and decision-making.
